Field Data Platform

When you have people in the field across many LGAs, reports come in late, in different formats and full of errors. The Field Data Platform gives your field team simple forms on their phones, gives supervisors a way to check and return reports, and gives management live numbers by state, LGA and ward.

The problem it solves

  • Reports arrive as WhatsApp messages, photos of paper and different spreadsheets.
  • Mistakes are found weeks later, after decisions have been made.
  • Management cannot see progress across locations in real time.
  • There is no record of who changed what.

What is included

Mobile forms

Forms that work in any phone browser, with dropdowns, required fields and checks that stop bad data at the source.

Location structure

Set up your coverage as state, LGA, ward and site. Import thousands of locations at once from a CSV file, with checks for duplicates.

Assignments

Assign each field agent to the sites they cover, so they only see and submit for their own areas.

Supervisor review

Supervisors check each submission and return it for correction with a note.

Live dashboard

Totals, progress and coverage by state, LGA and ward, updated as reports come in.

Exports

Download data as CSV or Excel for analysis and donor reports.

Secure access

Admin-issued login codes, two-factor login for admins and supervisors, and a full history of every edit.

Read-only viewer

Give leadership or partners a summary view without edit access.

This product is based on a system designed for a statewide deployment covering thousands of reporting sites.

Who it is for

  • NGOs and development programmes
  • Health and outreach campaigns
  • Survey and research firms
  • FMCG distributors and sales teams
  • Monitoring and evaluation teams
