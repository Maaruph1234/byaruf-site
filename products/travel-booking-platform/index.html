Travel Booking Platform

Travel agencies lose bookings when clients cannot see packages online, cannot pay in parts or keep calling to ask about their visa. The Travel Booking Platform gives your agency a professional website that sells, a portal where clients follow their trip and an admin side where staff manage everything.

The problem it solves

  • Packages are shared as flyers on WhatsApp and get lost in chats.
  • Instalment payments for Hajj and Umrah are tracked on paper.
  • Clients call repeatedly for updates on visas and travel dates.
  • Staff tasks like passport collection and visa filing are not tracked.

What is included

Public website

A fast, mobile-friendly website for your agency with package pages, photos, prices, inclusions and departure dates.

Package management

Create and update Hajj, Umrah, tour, visa and ticketing packages without calling a developer.

Online booking

Clients choose a package, add travellers and submit a booking from their phone.

Instalment payments

Set deposit and balance schedules. Clients and staff see what has been paid and what is due.

Client portal

Each client logs in to see their booking, upload documents, download receipts and follow visa and travel updates.

Staff task board

Assign tasks like passport collection, visa filing and ticket issuance, with due dates and status.

Booking pipeline

See every booking from enquiry to confirmed to travelled.

Reports

Sales by package, seats filled, outstanding balances and staff performance.

This product grew out of a full travel platform specification prepared for a Nigerian Hajj, Umrah and travel company.
