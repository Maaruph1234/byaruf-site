About ByAruf

What is ByAruf?

ByAruf (BYARUF Networks & Labs) is a software and technology business based in Kubwa, Abuja. We sell ready-made business software, build custom web and mobile apps, develop blockchain solutions, set up AI automations, manage tech projects and do business branding. It is run by Ma'aruf Yusuf, a telecoms engineer and Solidity-certified blockchain developer.

Who is Ma'aruf Yusuf?

Ma'aruf Yusuf is the founder of ByAruf. He has a B.Eng in Telecommunications Engineering from the Air Force Institute of Technology (AFIT), Kaduna, holds AWS certifications and is a Solidity-certified blockchain developer. He was previously COO at dGlobalIT and is a Program Officer at Evergreen Lifecare Foundation.

Where are you located?

We are based in Kubwa, Bwari Area Council, Abuja, Federal Capital Territory. We work with clients across Abuja, including Wuse, Maitama, Garki, Gwarinpa, Jabi, Lugbe and Lokogoma, and across Nigeria and abroad. Most work is done remotely, and in-person meetings in Abuja can be arranged.

Do you work with clients outside Abuja?

Yes. We can work with clients in Lagos, Kano, Kaduna, Port Harcourt and any other state, as well as clients outside Nigeria. Meetings, demos and handover happen over video call, WhatsApp and email.

Products

What products do you sell?

We have four ready-made products: the Property Portfolio Tracker for real estate firms, the Travel Booking Platform for travel agencies, the Field Data Platform for NGOs and field teams, and the Courier and Delivery App for logistics businesses. Each one is set up with your brand and data.

Do you have software for real estate companies?

Yes. The Property Portfolio Tracker manages estates, plots and units, buyer records, instalment payment plans, overdue alerts, receipts, allocation letters and management reports. It started as a nine-module system for a real estate company in Abuja.

Do you have software for travel agencies?

Yes. The Travel Booking Platform gives you a website to sell packages, online booking with instalment payments, a client portal for documents and updates, and an admin side with a staff task board and reports. It suits Hajj and Umrah operators, tour companies and visa consultants.

Do you have software for NGOs or field teams?

Yes. The Field Data Platform gives field agents mobile forms, lets supervisors review and return reports, and shows management live dashboards by state, LGA and ward, with CSV and Excel exports and secure login.

Can you build a delivery or courier app?

Yes. The Courier and Delivery App includes a customer app, a rider app, live tracking, pricing rules, payments and rider earnings, and an admin dashboard. It runs on Android and iPhone and is published under your brand.

Can the product be customised for my business?

Yes. Every setup includes your logo, colours, staff accounts and existing records. Small changes to fit how you work are included. Bigger new features are quoted separately.

Services

Do you do blockchain development?

Yes. We build Solidity smart contracts, ERC-20 tokens, NFTs, decentralised apps with wallet connection, and tamper-proof record systems on Ethereum and other EVM chains. Every contract is tested on a test network before it goes live.

What AI automations can you set up?

We set up website and WhatsApp chatbots, automatic payment and appointment reminders, document data extraction, automated reports and connections between apps like Google Sheets, email and CRMs. We can also add AI features to your existing software.

Can you manage my tech project?

Yes. We scope your project, help choose and manage developers or vendors, set milestones and budgets, test what is delivered and handle rollout. We can also step into a stalled project and get it moving again.

Do you do branding and logo design?

Yes. We design logos and brand identities, company profiles, proposals, pitch decks, business stationery and social media templates, plus a matching website.

Do you build websites?

Yes. We build business websites, e-commerce stores with Paystack or Flutterwave, web apps, dashboards and client portals. Sites are fast, mobile-friendly and set up for Google search.

Do you build mobile apps?

Yes. We build Android and iPhone apps with React Native from one codebase, and publish them on Google Play and the App Store under your name.

Do you offer cloud or hosting services?

Yes. We are AWS certified and set up hosting, migrations, backups, monitoring, security and cost control. Where a simpler platform fits better, we will recommend it.

The monthly plan covers hosting, daily backups, security updates, bug fixes and support by WhatsApp and email.
