About ByAruf and Ma'aruf Yusuf

ByAruf (BYARUF Networks & Labs) is a software and technology business in Kubwa, Abuja. We turn real client projects into products other businesses can use straight away, and we take on custom work where a product does not fit.

The founder

Ma'aruf Yusuf is a telecommunications engineer and Solidity-certified blockchain developer. He studied Telecommunications Engineering at the Air Force Institute of Technology (AFIT), Kaduna, and holds AWS certifications.

He served as COO of dGlobalIT, works as a Program Officer at Evergreen Lifecare Foundation and has long community leadership experience with Smiling Faces Empowerment Initiative. That mix of engineering, operations and community work shapes how ByAruf builds: practical systems that real teams can use.

His research includes SpectraChain, an EVM-based framework for sharing radio spectrum between Nigerian telecom operators, presented at IEEE NIGERCON 2026.
