Courier and Delivery App

Dispatch and logistics businesses grow fast but often run on phone calls and WhatsApp. The Courier and Delivery App gives your customers a branded app to book and track deliveries, gives riders an app to accept and complete jobs, and gives you a dashboard to run the whole operation.

The problem it solves

  • Orders come in by phone and WhatsApp and get missed.
  • Customers keep calling to ask where their parcel is.
  • Rider earnings and cash collections are hard to reconcile.
  • There is no history to resolve complaints.

What is included

Customer app

Customers book pickups, enter drop-off details, see the price before they confirm and pay.

Rider app

Riders receive jobs, accept them, update status and capture proof of delivery.

Live tracking

Customers and admins follow each delivery from pickup to drop-off.

Pricing rules

Set prices by distance, zone, parcel size or flat rates for areas like Wuse, Kubwa or Lugbe.

Payments and earnings

Track payments, cash on delivery and rider earnings in one place.

Admin dashboard

Assign jobs, manage riders, view all deliveries and handle complaints.

Notifications

Customers get updates at each stage of the delivery.

Your brand

Published on Google Play and the App Store under your business name and logo.

Built with React Native and Expo, so one codebase runs on both Android and iPhone.

Who it is for

  • Dispatch and courier companies
  • Restaurants and food vendors
  • Pharmacies and supermarkets
  • E-commerce sellers
  • Laundry and errand services

Available to businesses in Abuja, the FCT and anywhere in Nigeria.
