The problem it solves

  • Instalment payments are tracked by hand, so overdue buyers are noticed late.
  • Receipts, allocation letters and deeds are scattered across email, phones and cabinets.
  • Nobody can quickly answer how many units are sold, reserved or still available.
  • Staff turnover means customer history walks out the door.

What is included

Property and estate listings

Add estates, plots, blocks and units with size, price, status and location. See at a glance what is available, reserved, sold or under allocation.

Buyer records

Every buyer gets a profile with contact details, next of kin, the units they bought and a full timeline of every interaction.

Payment plans and instalments

Set outright or instalment plans. The system works out each due date, records every payment and shows the balance left.

Overdue alerts

See every buyer who is behind, by how much and for how long, so your team can follow up the same day.

Receipts and documents

Generate receipts and store allocation letters, contracts, survey plans and deeds against each buyer and unit.

Sales pipeline

Track leads from first enquiry to inspection, offer and sale, with the staff member responsible for each one.

Reports for management

Sales, collections, outstanding balances and inventory by estate, by month and by staff member. Export to Excel.

Staff roles and permissions

Admins, sales staff and accountants each see only what they need. Every change is logged.

Dashboard

A live overview of units sold, money collected this month and buyers who need attention.

This product started as a nine-module system built for a real estate company in Abuja and is in active use.
